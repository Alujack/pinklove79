79 Children, 79 Stories, One Hope

Behind the number 79 are 79 children, each with a story, a family, and a dream for the future.

These children come from different and difficult circumstances. Some are growing up in families living in poverty. Some come from families who survive by collecting recyclable materials. Some have experienced the separation or divorce of their parents. Some have parents who are serving prison sentences and are unable to provide the care their children need. Others are being cared for by elderly grandparents or relatives who have very limited resources.

Their circumstances are different, but they share something in common:

They deserve a chance.

They deserve to have enough food to eat.

They deserve to go to school.

They deserve clean clothes and school supplies.

They deserve healthcare and someone who cares when they are struggling.

And most importantly, they deserve to grow up knowing that they are not forgotten.

That is why we support these 79 children.

We provide help with their daily meals, education, school supplies, clothing, healthcare, and other essential needs. But our support is about more than providing things. We want to create a safe and caring environment where children can learn, grow, build confidence, and look toward the future with hope.

❤️ Why We Care

We believe that a difficult beginning does not have to determine a child's future.

A child may be born into poverty, but poverty should not take away their dreams.

A child may come from a broken family, but they still deserve love and encouragement.

A child may have parents who cannot care for them, but they should still have people around them who believe in them.

We cannot change everything in their lives, but together, we can change something.

A meal can give a child strength for another day of learning.

A school bag can help a child walk into school with confidence.

An education can open a door to a different future.

And a caring community can remind a child that they matter.

🌱 Building a Better Future

Our hope is that these children will not simply receive help today, but will have opportunities to become healthy, educated, confident, and independent adults tomorrow.

We invite caring people from around the world to join us in this journey.

You don't have to change the whole world to make a difference.

Sometimes, helping one child is enough to change one life.

And when we come together, 79 children can have 79 more reasons to hope.

79 children. 79 stories. One community. One hope for a better future.



Supporting 500 Villagers and Their Families

Our community work extends beyond supporting children. We also want to help around 500 villagers and their families who are facing financial difficulties and limited opportunities.

Many families in rural communities depend on small farming, agricultural work, or daily labor. With limited capital, skills, and resources, it can be difficult to create a stable source of income.

That is why we want to provide practical support that can help families build a more stable, independent, and dignified life.

🌱 Lemongrass Farming & Small Agriculture

One of our community initiatives is to help families start or expand small lemongrass farms and other suitable agricultural crops.

Depending on each family's needs, support may include:

- 🌱 Lemongrass seeds and planting materials
- 🌾 Other suitable small agricultural crops
- 🧑‍🌾 Basic farming tools and supplies
- 💧 Support for basic water and irrigation needs
- 💰 Small starting capital
- 📚 Basic farming knowledge and guidance
- 🛒 Helping connect farmers with local markets
- 🤝 Where appropriate, purchasing their agricultural products to help create a reliable market

Our goal is to help families grow, sell, earn, and gradually become more self-reliant.

🛍️ Helping Families Start Small Businesses

Not every family has suitable land for farming, and not everyone wants to become a farmer.

Some people may have the ability to earn a living through small businesses or selling products. We want to help them develop practical skills and, where appropriate, provide small start-up support.

For example, we may help families explore opportunities such as:

- 👕 Small mobile clothing-selling businesses
- 👟 Mobile shoe-selling businesses
- 🛍️ Small household-goods businesses
- 🥬 Selling vegetables or agricultural products
- 🍚 Small food or local-product businesses
- 🧺 Other simple businesses suitable for their community

We can also encourage people to learn basic business skills, such as buying products, setting prices, keeping simple records, communicating with customers, and managing their small business.

The purpose is not simply to give money. It is to help create an opportunity to earn an income.

📚 Skills for a Better Future

We believe that skills can be just as valuable as financial support.

Where possible, we want to connect villagers with opportunities to learn useful skills that can help them find work or start something of their own.

This may include:

- Basic farming skills
- Small-business skills
- Selling and customer-service skills
- Basic financial management
- Handicrafts and practical skills
- Food preparation and small food businesses
- Other locally useful vocational skills

Our hope is that a person who learns a skill today can use that skill to support their family for many years.

🏠 Helping Families Live with Dignity

We also recognize that a family's quality of life depends on more than income.

When there is a genuine need, we hope to support families with practical household necessities such as:

- 🌀 Fans
- 🍚 Rice cookers and basic cooking equipment
- 💧 Clean drinking-water dispensers and related water supplies
- 🏠 Other essential household items

These may be simple things, but for families with very limited resources, they can make everyday life safer, healthier, and more comfortable.

❤️ From Assistance to Opportunity

Our vision is not to create long-term dependence.

We want to help people build a path forward.

A family may receive small capital to start a lemongrass farm.

Another family may receive support to begin selling clothes or shoes.

Someone else may learn a practical skill that helps them find work.

Every family has different needs, abilities, and dreams.

Our role is to listen, encourage, and provide support where we can.

🌍 Our Vision for 500 Villagers

We hope to support approximately 500 villagers and their families through a combination of:

🌱 Lemongrass & Agriculture
💰 Small Start-up Capital
🛍️ Small Business Opportunities
📚 Skills & Training
💧 Clean Drinking Water
🏠 Basic Household Support
🤝 Community Care

We believe that true community development is not only about giving someone something today.

It is about helping them gain the skills, opportunity, confidence, and resources to build a better tomorrow.

One family can become stronger.
One small business can support a household.
One farm can create income.
One skill can open a door.
And when many families move forward together, an entire community can grow.

❤️ 500 Villagers. Many Stories. One Hope.

We may not be able to solve every problem, but together we can make a meaningful difference—one family, one opportunity, and one village at a time.



One Church — A Place to Learn, Grow and Serve

Our community also runs one local church that welcomes children, families, and community members who wish to come together to learn and grow in faith.

The church provides a place where people can learn about the Bible, understand Christian teachings, pray together, and encourage one another.

📖 Learning About the Bible

We provide opportunities for children and adults to learn about the Bible in a simple and understandable way.

Through Bible lessons, discussions, activities, and community gatherings, we encourage people to discover teachings about:

- ❤️ Love and compassion
- 🤝 Helping and caring for others
- 🙏 Faith and prayer
- 🌱 Forgiveness and personal growth
- 🕊️ Peace and kindness
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Respect for family and community
- ❤️ Serving people who are in need

For children especially, we want learning to be safe, welcoming, and encouraging, giving them an opportunity to learn positive values while growing together with others.

🏠 A Place for the Community

The church is more than a place for worship.

It is also a place where community members can come together, build friendships, encourage one another, and learn how to serve their community.

We hope it can become a place where people feel welcomed, respected, cared for, and supported.

🌍 Growing Together

Our vision is to bring children, families, and community members together to learn, share, serve, and grow.

Through the church and our wider community projects, we hope to encourage people to care for one another and make a positive difference in the lives of others.

One Church.
One Community.
Many Lives.
A shared hope for a better future.
